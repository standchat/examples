# Image-generation provenance

All Halloween artwork described below was generated for this project with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-10-09. The characters are original designs, not Rivian or licensed franchise characters.

## Updated character packs

The fifteen runtime characters were generated as separate transparent images, replacing the earlier code-drawn SVG illustrations. Each pack has an original ghost, pumpkin and bat. Full prompts, original generated file paths and destinations are recorded in these files:

- [G and PG prompts](../assets/provenance-g-pg.json): soft preschool and playful storybook styles.
- [PG-13 prompts](../assets/provenance-pg-13.json): gauze apparition, weathered carved gourd, realistic leathery bat.
- [R prompts](../assets/provenance-r.json): creature-feature practical effects.
- [NC-17 prompts](../assets/provenance-nc-17.json): theatrical haunted-house monsters.

The original generated PNGs are preserved in the workspace. The page and marketing use full-size WebP copies; the downloadable runtime uses separately resized 384 × 384 WebPs from `assets/runtime/`. Both preserve alpha. The character art is displayed without recoloring or programmatic redrawing.

`linkedin-01-familiar.png` uses the actual updated PG characters. `linkedin-03-choice.png` compares the actual five updated ghosts. Both are browser-rendered compositions from [`source.html`](source.html). [`../cast.html`](../cast.html) shows the complete fifteen-character set with selectable light and dark backgrounds.

## Original paper-theatre prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original hero illustration for Stand Chat Halloween mode, used on an amber website and creative campaign.
Primary request: a beautifully handcrafted Halloween paper theatre in which an ivory friendly ghost, an orange jack-o-lantern and a charcoal bat have come alive and escaped from a miniature browser window. The idea is familiar web pages made delightfully haunted.
Scene/backdrop: flat warm pumpkin amber studio backdrop (#f4a23c), seamless surface, gorgeous tactile cut paper diorama. One off-white browser window slab sits in perspective with a simple top chrome strip, tiny three window dots, and a few quiet graphite placeholder shapes suggesting a website. No legible words anywhere.
Subject: expressive floating ivory paper ghost smiling and waving, dimensional folded orange pumpkin with a warm carved face, one cutout charcoal bat in motion. Clever theatrical shadows underneath the characters; delightfully strange, mischievous but welcoming. Two small black four-point star cutouts only as accents. Ghost has two small ink eyes, a small curious mouth, graceful folded bottom.
Style/medium: sophisticated editorial paper sculpture, vintage Halloween paper ephemera meets contemporary design studio product photography; real thick matte craft-paper edges, folded forms, subtle fibers, crisply photographed. Original characters, no franchise likenesses.
Composition/framing: 3:2 landscape, full diorama visible, composition centered with a generous unbroken amber margin around all objects. Browser is wide and lies at a dynamic oblique angle occupying lower-middle, ghost escapes above-right, pumpkin left foreground, bat upper left. Strong silhouette, minimal enough to understand instantly.
Lighting/mood: warm directional side lighting with long deep charcoal shadows, warm friendly theatrical mood; no fog or smoke.
Color palette: orange pumpkin amber, warm ivory, dark ink, tiny muted sage leaf.
Constraints: no typography, no brands, no logos, no CTA, no watermarks, no glow, no glossy plastic, no faux glass, no gradients, no generic emoji, no horror gore, no clutter. Image should look like a high-end independent design magazine cover photograph.
