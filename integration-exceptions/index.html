HOW IT WORKS / COPY THE PATTERN
A conversation is a path through the workflow.
Follow the stopped record
  1. The opening trace is an illustrative trial record stopped by the paid-customer rule. Click plan = trial to quote that field.
  2. Ask your question at step 02. A real Stand AI reply continues the vertical trace. On your own site, a configured human can take over in that same path.
  3. Change the exception’s failure branch to Send to team review and replay. The comparison shows what changed relative to the investigation’s original run.
  4. Choose to include that replay, then send a follow-up. The before/after evidence travels with your question. Replies never execute a change.
